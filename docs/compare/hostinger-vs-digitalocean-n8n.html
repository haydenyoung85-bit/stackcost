Verdict

Hostinger, by a wide margin at 4 GB. Hostinger's n8n plan APP 1 (1 vCPU / 4 GB) is $4.99/mo intro and $9.99/mo at renewal on a 24-month prepay; a DigitalOcean 4 GB Basic Droplet is $24/mo, billed hourly with no term (checked 2026-11-23).

  • Pick Hostinger if you know you’ll keep n8n running for 2 years: even its renewal price beats DigitalOcean.
  • Pick DigitalOcean to try n8n with no commitment, or if you want hourly scaling and its tutorials.

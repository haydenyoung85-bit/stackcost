Hetzner vs DigitalOcean for n8n

For 4 GB of RAM, Hetzner's Regular Performance CPX22 (2 vCPU/4 GB) is €19.99/mo excl. VAT in EU locations vs $24/mo for DigitalOcean's 4 GB Basic droplet (checked 2026-10-26). Hetzner's cheaper Cost-Optimized CX23 (€5.99/mo) was marked not available. Hetzner's US locations use separate, pricier plans, and n8n isn't in its one-click app list; DigitalOcean has more regions and a larger marketplace.

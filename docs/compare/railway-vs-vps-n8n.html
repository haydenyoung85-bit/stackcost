Verdict

Railway bills usage: $10.01 per GB of RAM and $20.01 per vCPU for a full 30-day month, and the Hobby plan is $5/mo including $5 of usage. Our estimate for a small always-on n8n is $7.16/mo, and about $14.32/mo with Postgres (rates checked 2026-11-23; your real bill depends on usage).

  • Pick Railway if you want n8n live in minutes from a template and don’t want to manage a server.
  • Pick a VPS if n8n runs 24/7: a fixed price with no surprise usage bill, and more RAM for the money.

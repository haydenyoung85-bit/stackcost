Verdict

Contabo. Its Cloud VPS 10 n8n plan (4 vCPU / 8 GB) is $5.28/mo for the first 24 months and lists $6.60/mo regular. Hetzner's 8 GB CX33 is €8.99/mo excl. VAT but was marked not available; its orderable 4 GB CPX22 is €19.99/mo (checked 2026-11-09).

  • Pick Contabo for the most RAM per dollar and a one-click n8n install, if a 24-month term is fine.
  • Pick Hetzner for no-term hourly billing and NVMe disks, if you can live with fewer GB per euro.

Contabo’s prices are “incl. applicable taxes” per its page; its “renewal” is the struck-through regular price, not explicitly labelled as renewal. Contabo source, checked 2026-11-09.

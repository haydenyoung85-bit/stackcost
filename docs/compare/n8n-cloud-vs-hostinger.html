Verdict

Hostinger. n8n Cloud Starter is $24/mo ($20/mo billed yearly) for 2,500 executions; Hostinger KVM 1 (1 vCPU / 4 GB) is $6.49/mo intro and $11.99/mo at renewal, with no execution cap from n8n (checked 2026-11-16). Over a year that is $240 vs $143.88, even at Hostinger's renewal price.

  • Pick Hostinger if you’re OK running a server: no execution cap, and even its renewal price is well below n8n Cloud Starter.
  • Pick n8n Cloud if you want zero maintenance and stay under 2,500 runs a month.
